# Citizen Issue Reporting

## Overview

Citizen Issue Reporting is an AI-powered platform designed to improve communication between citizens and public institutions.

Every day, citizens encounter problems in their communities such as damaged roads, broken streetlights, waste accumulation, water leaks, unsafe public spaces, and other issues that affect daily life. Unfortunately, reporting these problems is often difficult, slow, or ineffective.

Our solution allows citizens to report issues quickly and efficiently while leveraging Artificial Intelligence to help authorities prioritize and respond faster.

---

## Problem Statement

Many community issues remain unresolved because citizens do not have an easy and centralized way to report them.

Current reporting processes are often:

* Slow
* Manual
* Difficult to track
* Poorly organized

As a result, important issues may go unnoticed or take too long to be addressed.

---

## Solution

Citizen Issue Reporting provides a simple and intelligent platform where citizens can report problems directly from their smartphones or computers.

The platform combines user reports with AI analysis to automatically classify issues, generate professional reports, and determine their level of urgency.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1: Detect an Issue

A citizen notices a problem in the community such as:

* Road damage
* Broken streetlight
* Water leak
* Illegal dumping
* Public safety concern
* Environmental issue

### Step 2: Submit a Report

The citizen:

* Takes a photo
* Adds a description
* Provides the location
* Submits the report

### Step 3: AI Analysis

The AI automatically analyzes the submitted information and:

* Identifies the type of issue
* Generates a structured report
* Assigns a severity level
* Determines the priority
* Suggests the responsible department

Example:

Issue Detected: Road Damage

Category: Infrastructure

Priority: High

Suggested Department: Public Works

Recommended Action: Repair within 7 days

### Step 4: Monitoring Dashboard

Authorities can access a dashboard showing:

* New reports
* Issue categories
* Priority levels
* Issue status
* Community statistics

Statuses include:

* Pending
* In Progress
* Resolved
